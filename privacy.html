Chronos privacy policy

Last updated 27 September 2026.

Chronos is a personal application used only by its developer, Ryan Norris. It is not available to other users.

What Chronos accesses

With the account owner's permission, Chronos reads and updates the Google Calendar data of the signed-in account, to plan and schedule the owner's own time.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is stored on the owner's own computer and used only to provide planning features to the owner.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • To generate plans and suggestions, relevant excerpts may be processed by the AI service the owner uses (Anthropic's Claude) under the owner's own account.

Google user data

Chronos's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; locally stored data is then deleted by the owner.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/ryannorris14/chronos-app.
